five_o_poker: renderer for Five-O Poker — a 2-player heads-up game where each player builds five 5-card columns and beats their opponent in the most columns. Adds `FiveOPokerReplay` (replay playback) and the interactive `FiveOPokerBoard` (`onAction`/`humanSeat` for live human-vs-bot play, clicking a shortest column to `place`), with `detectGameId` recognising the game and the 4th-card-face-down showdown view.
